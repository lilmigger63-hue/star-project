Fresh read-only whole-branch review and focused PAL coordinate-space review reported no Critical or Important findings. No proprietary game assets or optional commercial/unverified downloads were imported. See [ASSET_CREDITS.md](ASSET_CREDITS.md) for exact files, authors, licenses, hashes and conversion limits.

Representative actual fixture screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/0.10.0`; source downloads remain outside the checkout. Offline Mojang-service/audio/cursor warnings did not prevent the recorded checks. Unlimited levels, level-8 ×1.5 mod-XP rewards, separate point types, class evolution, personal UUID aura hue, gameplay costs/targets/timing, H/K bindings and HUD anchors remain.
